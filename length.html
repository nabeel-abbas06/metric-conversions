Metric Measurements Of Length

1. Meters (m)

A meter (or metre, outside the U.S.) is a basic unit of length in the metric system, which is used by most countries.

  • 1 meter = 100 centimeters
  • 1 meter = 1,000 millimeters
  • 1 meter = 3.28 feet

Examples:

  • A door is about 2 meters tall
  • A guitar is about 1 meter long
  • One big step is roughly 1 meter

The meter is the standard unit scientists use to measure length.

2. Feet (ft)

A feet is a unit of length used mainly in the United States and a few other countries.

  • 1 foot = 12 inches
  • 1 foot = 0.3048 meters
  • 3 feet = 1 yard

Examples:

  • The length of a ruler is often 1 foot
  • A doorway is usually about 6-7 feet tall

The name comes from the human foot historically it was based on foot length.

3. Inches (in)

An inch is a unit of length used mainly in the United States, the UK, and a few other countries.

  • 1 inch = 2.54 centimeters
  • 12 inches = 1 foot

Examples:

  • The width of a finger is about 1 inch
  • A standard pencil is about 7-8 inches long
  • A phone screen might be 6 inches diagonally

4. Millimeters (mm)

A millimeter (written as mm) is a unit of length in the metric system.

  • 1 millimeter = one-thousandth of a meter
  • 10 millimeters = 1 centimeter
  • 1,000 millimeters = 1 meter

Used for very small distances like:

  • The thickness of a coin
  • The width of a wire
  • Small engineering parts

For example, a pencil tip is about 1-2 mm wide.

5. Yards (yd)

A yard (written as yd) is a unit of length in the imperial/US system.

  • 1 yard = 3 feet
  • 1 yard = 36 inches
  • 1 yard = 0.914 meters

Often used for:

  • Room length
  • Fabric
  • Sports fields

A football field is 100 yards long.

6. Miles (mi)

A mile is a unit of distance used mainly in the United States.

  • 1 mile = 5,280 feet
  • 1 mile = 1.609 kilometers
  • 1 mile = 1,760 yards

Statute miles are used on roads. Nautical miles are used in aviation and sea travel.

7. Kilometers (km)

A kilometer is a metric unit of distance used worldwide.

  • 1 kilometer = 1,000 meters
  • 1 kilometer = 0.62 miles

Examples:

  • A short walk is 1-2 km
  • A city drive might be 10 km
  • 5 km = a little over 3 miles
