Metric Measurements Of Mass

1. Kilograms (kg)

A kilogram is the base unit of mass in the metric system.

  • 1 kilogram = 1,000 grams
  • 1 kilogram = 2.204 pounds

Examples:

  • A bag of rice weighs about 5 kg
  • A laptop weighs around 2 kg

2. Grams (g)

A gram is used to measure small masses.

  • 1 gram = 1,000 milligrams

Commonly used in cooking and science.

3. Milligrams (mg)

Used for very small quantities.

  • 1,000 mg = 1 gram

Used in medicine.

4. Pounds (lb)

A pound is commonly used in the United States.

  • 1 pound = 16 ounces
  • 1 pound = 0.4536 kg

5. Ounces (oz)

An ounce is a smaller imperial unit of mass.

  • 16 ounces = 1 pound

6. Metric Tons (t)

Used for very heavy masses.

  • 1 metric ton = 1,000 kg

Used in shipping and industry.
