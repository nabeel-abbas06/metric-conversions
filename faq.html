Frequently Asked Questions

Q1. How do I enter numbers in scientific notation?

Use formats like 1.2e3 for 1200.

Q2. What is a knot? What is a nautical mile?

a. Knot
A knot is a unit of speed used in maritime and aviation contexts.
1 knot = 1 nautical mile per hour.
Abbreviated as kn or kt.

b. Nautical Mile
1 nautical mile = 1.852 km = 1.1508 miles.

Q3. What about rainfall? How do I convert between inches and millimeters of rain?

1 inch = 25.4 mm
1 mm = 0.03937 in

Q4. What is the difference between the Short Ton, Long Ton, and Metric Ton?

Short Ton (US Ton): 1 short ton = 2,000 lb = 907.1847 kg

Long Ton (Imperial Ton): 1 long ton = 2,240 lb = 1,016.05 kg

Metric Ton (Tonne): 1 metric ton = 1,000 kg = 2,204.62 lb

Q5. At what temperature are Celsius and Fahrenheit the same?

Celsius and Fahrenheit are equal at -40°.
