Metric Measurements Of Temperature

1. Celsius (°C)

Celsius is the most commonly used temperature scale worldwide.

  • 0°C = freezing point of water
  • 100°C = boiling point of water

Used in weather reports and daily life.

2. Fahrenheit (°F)

Fahrenheit is mainly used in the United States.

  • 32°F = freezing point of water
  • 212°F = boiling point of water

3. Kelvin (K)

Kelvin is the SI unit of temperature used in science.

  • 0 K = absolute zero
  • No degree symbol is used

Commonly used in physics and chemistry.

Understanding temperature scales helps in science, weather forecasting, cooking, and engineering.
